# ASRD Responsible Use Policy

The Adversarial Surface-Form Robustness Dataset (ASRD) is licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSE). Access to the dataset on the Hugging Face Hub additionally requires agreement to this Responsible Use Policy.

## 1. Research and Defensive Use Only

ASRD is published for academic research, red-teaming evaluation, model alignment auditing, and AI safety benchmarking.

## 2. Prohibited Uses

You agree not to use, distribute, or operationalize the contents of this dataset for:

1. Developing, facilitating, or executing malicious software, cyberattacks, or network intrusions.
2. Obtaining or providing actionable instructions for synthesizing chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear (CBRN) agents or explosives.
3. Conducting automated or targeted harassment, doxxing, defamation, or discrimination.
4. Bypassing the safety filters of deployed systems with malicious intent.
5. Building or integrating offensive tooling.

## 3. Dual-Use Acknowledgment

ASRD contains harmful requests presented in non-canonical surface forms. It contains requests only and does not include instructions or answers to those requests. By accessing or using the dataset, you acknowledge that these requests are provided strictly for measuring model safety behavior and robustness to surface-form variation.

## 4. Redistribution

If you redistribute ASRD or material derived from it, please include this policy and point recipients to it.
